PROJECT 01 Academic Forum

Discussion Forum

An open academic dialogue space between members and invited guests on diverse topics, where every viewpoint is heard and challenged constructively.

Discussion Forum is Skillcetera's regular thematic panel series, connecting club members with experienced speakers, lecturers, and industry guests (covering Education, Employment, critical thinking, and career skills). It is the cradle for active listening and sharp reasoning.
Regular Thematic Panels Critical Thinking & Dialogue

PROJECT 02 Cultural & Historical Expedition

Scavenger Hunt

An urban exploration race across Saigon's culture, history, and geography through hands-on challenges requiring strong teamwork.

Scavenger Hunt takes participants out onto the streets of Saigon to decipher cryptic clues and challenges tied to the city's heritage and culture. Across 6 seasons, it blends physical teamwork with deep local discovery.
6 Seasons · 50+ Participants/Season 10+ Locations Across Saigon
View Scavenger Hunt Post (Facebook) ↗
PROJECT 03 Mindfulness & Growth

Meditation Retreat

A mindful retreat into nature to practice meditation, self-reflect, and nurture inner balance.

Amid vibrant study and event schedules, Meditation Retreat offers a serene pause for members and attendees to reconnect with nature, practice mindfulness, listen within, and share openly. Club records document 3 meaningful seasons.
3 Recorded Seasons Nature Immersion & Inner Balance
View Meditation Retreat Post (Facebook) ↗
PROJECT 04 Merchandise & Fundraising

S-Store

Fundraising merchandise conceived and designed by Skillcetera members: t-shirts, lanyards, tote bags, and badges.

S-Store is Skillcetera's official merchandise project where members conceptualize, design, and produce branded apparel and accessories (including collaborative drops with Coolmate). Over 500 lanyards, 100+ t-shirts, and 100+ tote bags sold.
Sold: 500+ Lanyards · 100+ Tees · 100+ Totes Designed by Members

PROJECT 05 Campus Relations & Media

The MoneyVerse

Partnering in financial literacy and investment education for youth produced by VTV Times as campus relations and student ambassador partners.

The MoneyVerse is a financial and investment education series combining entertainment and technology for youth, produced by VTV Times (Vietnam Television). Skillcetera collaborated in campus relations, student ambassador coordination, and club networking across 10 southern university campuses.
Scope: 10 Southern Campuses (VTV Times) Student Ambassador Coordination
