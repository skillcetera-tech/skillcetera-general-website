Skillcetera has 5 departments, each with a distinct role in running our events. Click any department to read the full job description and requirements.

Fully responsible for equipment, facilities, and logistics; the core team coordinating on the ground at major events including ESC and TEDxFPTUniversityHCMC.

Responsibilities

  • Know the inventory of equipment, facilities, and relationships with internal and external contractors and vendors.
  • Audit the quantity, location, and condition of borrowed equipment before and after each event.
  • Build detailed checklists and assign staff for event day.
  • Contribute ideas for onsite space setup.
  • Distribute gifts from the university and sponsors, ensuring correct delivery on event day.
  • Review and collect documentation and price quotes for event materials.
  • Support the Account team with planning and procedures for PDP office submissions.
  • Serve as the primary operational crew on the day of each event.

Requirements

  • Familiar with administrative documents and paperwork requirements.
  • Strong communicator; proactive in sourcing appropriate vendors.
  • High sense of responsibility, especially in the days leading up to an event.
  • Able to build structured checklists that keep events running smoothly.
  • High energy, good stamina, independent, and quick to adapt.
  • Detail-oriented, thorough, and honest in all tasks.

The strategic liaison between the club and institutional partners. Handles direct communication with the PDP office, payment processing, contracts, and care of speakers and organizing partners.

Responsibilities

  • Work with the PDP office: exchange project information, fulfill payment obligations, and process facility borrowing procedures.
  • Audit payment documents and contracts with partners to prevent errors.
  • Directly liaise with speakers, clubs, and external organizations to protect both parties' interests.
  • Step in to cover gaps in other departments when necessary.

Requirements

  • Able to write professional emails, reports, and project plans.
  • Able to create content and develop ideas for events and projects.
  • Strong presentation and public speaking skills.
  • Relevant experience is a plus.
Stakeholder Management Official Documentation Negotiation Presentation

Develops and sustains financial and in-kind resources; builds sponsorship proposals and negotiates long-term partnerships with businesses that support Skillcetera.

Responsibilities

  • Build and refine professional Sponsorship Proposals.
  • Research and identify potential sponsors suited to each project.
  • Develop and maintain sponsor relationships: negotiate, sign contracts, support on event day, and track deliverables.
  • Prepare all related documentation throughout the sponsorship process.
  • Monitor and ensure sponsor benefits are fulfilled throughout the partnership.
  • Coordinate closely with Admin, Account, and Event teams.

Requirements

  • Able to research and manage information effectively.
  • Persistent and eager to learn.
  • Communication, negotiation, and design skills are a plus.
Sponsorship Pitching Contract Negotiation CRM Partner Deliverables

The internal backbone of the club: manages member databases, upholds member benefits policies, runs recruitment, and sustains Skillcetera's culture of belonging.

Responsibilities

  • Manage member information and recruit new members and collaborators.
  • Continuously update the club database.
  • Communicate key communications tasks and event information to members.
  • Ensure member benefit policies are implemented fully and correctly.
  • Track and record member activity within the club.
  • Address issues and concerns among members.
  • Coordinate with the Event team to organize bonding events and retreats.

Requirements

  • Able to write clear emails, reports, and plans.
  • Detail-oriented and a strong communicator.
  • Able to manage databases.
  • Notion experience is a plus.
  • Basic web development knowledge is a plus.

The creative engine of Skillcetera: handles brand identity design, content strategy, photo production, and video editing across all club channels.

Designer Graphic & Brand Identity

Responsibilities

  • Design media collateral: social posts, banners, standees, merchandise, backdrops.
  • Create unique brand identity systems for each club event.

Requirements

  • Strong graphic design skills and design thinking.
  • Proficient in Canva.
  • Photoshop or Illustrator experience is a plus.

Content & Planning Strategy & Copywriting

Responsibilities

  • Build communications plans for each semester's club activity.
  • Develop marketing strategies and communication formats tailored to each event.
  • Write copy for posts across the club's channels.

Requirements

  • Proficient with Google Suite (Docs, Sheets).
  • Always up to date with trends; clear, creative writing voice.
  • Able to plan and manage communications calendars.

Production Photo & Video

Responsibilities

  • Photographer: shoot and post-process photos at club events.
  • Video Editor: edit videos for social platforms, ensuring quality and viewer engagement.

Requirements

  • Photographer: owns a camera, able to handle post-processing, passionate about visual storytelling.
  • Video Editor: proficient in at least one editing tool (Premiere Pro, DaVinci Resolve, CapCut); appreciates cinema; stays current with social media trends.
