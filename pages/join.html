04 DEPARTMENTS

Operating Departments at Skillcetera

Each member belongs to a core department while collaborating cross-functionally across large campaigns like ESC or TEDxFPTUniversityHCMC.

DEPARTMENT 01 Event Organizing

Event Organizing Department

Brainstorming program concepts, coordinating stage production, managing logistics, and executing risk management for ESC, TEDxFPTUniversityHCMC, and internal community events.

Skills: Coordination, Risk Management Stage & Event Production
DEPARTMENT 02 Media & Design

Media & Design Department

Building visual identity systems, producing media publications, documenting event photography, and managing the club's official social media channels.

Skills: Graphic Design, Photo/Video Brand Management
DEPARTMENT 03 Content & Academic

Content & Academic Department

Developing academic themes for Discussion Forum, authoring ESC competition prompts, working alongside TEDx speakers, and curating bilingual content.

Skills: Storytelling, Research, English Academic Editorial
DEPARTMENT 04 External Relations & HR

External Relations & HR Department

Connecting sponsor partners, guest speakers, inter-university networks, and nurturing member bonding through Meditation Retreat and Scavenger Hunt.

Skills: Negotiation, Networking, HR Partnership & Community
