ACADEMIC FRAMEWORK

The 3-Round Challenge Journey

Each round of ESC is designed to rigorously evaluate language mastery, depth of critical reasoning, and impromptu debate before an expert academic judging panel.

ROUND 01 Perspective Screening

Preliminary Round — Perspective Screening

Contestants submit entries expressing independent perspectives on the season's open theme. The academic panel evaluates argumentation structure, linguistic fluidity, and critical insight.

200+ Contestants Thinking & Language Evaluation
ROUND 02 Presentation & Defense

Semi-Final Round — Presentation & Defense

Outstanding contestants advance to on-stage keynote presentations followed by live cross-examination from academic judges, alongside specialized public speaking mentorship.

Intensive Mentoring Live Q&A Defense
ROUND 03 Grand Final Show

Grand Final Show — Championship Showdown

A high-caliber arena featuring top finalists competing through keynote orations and 1v1 debate face-offs before a live audience to crown the season champion.
