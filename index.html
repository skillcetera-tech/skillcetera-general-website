About Skillcetera

Skills and Languages Club at FPT University Ho Chi Minh City — where ideas are heard and large-scale experiences are created together.

Skillcetera is a vibrant event-organizing and academic hub empowering students through active knowledge exchange, professional execution, and lifelong community bonds.

01

Consistency is Flexibility

Building a knowledge-sharing community daily, using consistency as the foundation to unlock flexible adaptability.

02

Event Organizing Excellence

Organizing meticulous, engaging, educational, and inspiring events that reach far beyond university campus grounds.

03

Open & Inclusive Environment

An environment that respects diversity, encourages open dialogue, and stands together so that every member is heard and empowered.

COMMUNITY EXPERIENCES · 05 INITIATIVES

Much more
to discover together.

From academic forums and urban exploration races to mindful retreats, fundraising merchandise, and nationwide media partnerships.

01 Discussion Forum

An open academic dialogue space between members and invited guests on diverse topics, where every viewpoint is heard and challenged constructively.

Academic Forum · Periodic dialogues with members & guests
02 Scavenger Hunt

An urban exploration race across Saigon's culture, history, and geography through hands-on challenges requiring strong teamwork.

6 seasons organized · 50+ participants per season · 10+ locations across Saigon
03 Meditation Retreat

A mindful retreat into nature to practice meditation, self-reflect, and nurture inner balance.

3 recorded seasons · Nature immersion & personal growth
04 S-Store

Fundraising merchandise conceived and designed by Skillcetera members: t-shirts, lanyards, tote bags, and badges.

Sold: 500+ lanyards · 100+ t-shirts · 100+ tote bags (per source archive)

Partnering in financial literacy and investment education for youth produced by VTV Times as campus relations and student ambassador partners.
